3 Approaches for the Marketplace Screen

How should the 2nd Hand / Equipment Rental toggle be structured?

A

Pill Toggle at Top ★ Recommended

Two pill buttons sit at the top of the screen. Tap switches between 2nd Hand list and Equipment Rental grid. A "+" button in the top-right opens the post form. Matches your description exactly.

Landing Page with Two Cards

Marketplace tab shows a landing with two large tappable cards — "2nd Hand" and "Equipment Rental". Each navigates to its own screen. Cleaner separation but one extra tap.
