Which icon style for the tab bar?

The app already has expo-symbols installed. Here are 3 approaches — pick the feel you want.

Clean Line Icons ★ Recommended

Thin, minimal SVG-style line drawings. Elegant and modern — matches the dark card aesthetic. Uses expo-symbols (SF Symbols on iOS, Material on Android/web).
