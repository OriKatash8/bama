4. WHAT BUMPING CURRENT_TERMS_VERSION DOES
------------------------------------------
Where: src/core/constants/legal.ts, currently '1.0' (it was never bumped; the
documents went 1.1 -> 1.3 while it stayed '1.0', so every stored acceptance
reads termsVersion "1.0").

Mechanism (client-side only; no server or rules check):
  - needsConsent(user) is true when users/{uid}.termsVersion is missing or
    numerically lower than CURRENT_TERMS_VERSION ("1.4" > "1.0").
  - The onboarding gate (useOnboardingGate, nextAuthRoute, mode-select) sends
    such a user to /(auth)/consent before anything else. They must tick both
    boxes; Continue writes termsAcceptedAt, termsVersion, ageConfirmed,
    ageConfirmedAt. Cancel signs them out.

Who would be asked: EVERY signed-in user, clients and professionals alike,
because everyone stored "1.0". There is no way to ask professionals only.

When: only in an app build (or OTA update) that contains the new constant. A
phone on an older build never asks.

Relevant to the decision: your own Terms say a material change takes effect
30 days after notice (23.2) and that on a material update users are asked to
accept the new version in the app (23.3). The new 12.8 (an automatic
restriction without a warning) and the fee rename may count as material. That
is a legal call for you; I have not bumped anything.
