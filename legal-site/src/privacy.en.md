1.1. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information is collected when you use the BAMA app (the "App"), how it is collected, what it is used for, who it is shared with, how long it is kept, and how you can exercise your rights regarding it.

1.2. The App is operated by Roi Hamm, a licensed exempt dealer (Osek Patur), registration no. 209899822, of HaHadarim St., Kadima-Zoran, Israel (the "Operator", "we" or "us"). The Operator is the controller of the database for the purposes of the Protection of Privacy Law, 5741‑1981 (the "Privacy Protection Law"). Our contact details are set out in chapter 13.

1.3. This Policy forms an integral part of the App's Terms of Use (the "Terms"). Terms not defined here have the meaning given to them in the Terms.

1.4. When you register, you will be asked to expressly accept the Terms and this Policy. You are not legally required to provide us with information, and doing so depends on your free will and consent. However, without some details — such as your name, email address and phone number — we cannot open an account for you or provide the service or parts of it.

1.5. This Policy also applies to the BAMA web pages on which the Terms and this Policy are displayed.

### 2.3. Location — clarification

2.3.1. The App does not request location permission and does not collect your device's location.

2.3.2. Matching between Clients and Professionals is based only on the service area and project location entered manually.

2.3.3. Photos and videos you upload may contain data created by the camera device, including location data. We recommend checking this before uploading.

### 2.4. Information we do not intentionally collect

We do not ask for and do not wish to receive information of special sensitivity as defined in the Privacy Protection Law — such as information about health, origin, political opinions, religious beliefs or criminal record. Please avoid providing such information in free-text fields, messages or your portfolio.

## 3. How We Use Information

3.1. Operating the service — creating and managing your account, displaying profiles, matching Clients and Professionals by roles, skills and area, managing Projects, Offers and Engagements, completing Engagements (including automatic completion on the end date), operating chat, groups, Communities, the Store and Courses, and the rating mechanism.

3.2. Verification and preventing duplicate accounts — verifying your phone number (once enabled), ensuring one account per person, and enforcing the active Engagement limit.

3.3. Phone number disclosure — showing your phone number to the other party to an Engagement, only upon its completion, as set out in section 4.3.

3.4. Operational communications — verification codes (once enabled), notifications and messages about Projects, Offers, new messages, Engagement Completion, payment requests and system updates.

3.5. Brokerage Fee — calculating the fee, sending payment requests, issuing billing documents, handling disputes, refunds and non-payment, and bookkeeping.

3.6. Security, enforcement and fraud prevention — detecting suspicious activity, preventing impersonation and block evasion, looking into reports, disputes and disagreements, and enforcing the Terms. For these purposes only, the Operator's team may review the content of conversations, as set out in section 11.2 of the Terms.

3.7. Support — responding to requests and handling faults and requests.

3.8. Product improvement — detecting faults and improving the service based on user requests and data already in the system. Where possible, we use aggregated data for this.

3.9. Marketing — sending updates and marketing content, only with your consent and in accordance with section 30A of the Communications (Telecommunications and Broadcasting) Law, 5742‑1982. You may withdraw your consent at any time.

3.10. Legal compliance — meeting legal obligations, including tax and record-keeping obligations, and responding to requests from competent authorities or court orders.

3.11. Protecting rights — establishing, exercising or defending legal claims.

## 4. Information Visible to Other Users

4.1. Public information in the App — display name, profile photo, roles and skills, portfolio, service areas, price list (if published), rating and reviews received, Store listings you posted, and course listings. A review you wrote is shown with your display name.

4.2. Project Group — the Client and all Professionals hired for the Project see the display names and profiles of the group members and the messages and files sent in the group.

4.3. Phone number — is not public and is not searchable. It is shown only to the other party to an Engagement, mutually, upon its completion, for continued contact regarding payment and follow-up questions.

4.4. Communities — content you post in a Community is visible to its members. Community managers appointed by the Operator may manage and remove content in the Community, in accordance with the Terms.

4.5. We do not control what other users do with information disclosed to them. Sharing details in messages or in your profile is your responsibility. Do not post in public fields any information you do not want disclosed.

4.6. Using Users' details for any purpose beyond the specific Engagement — including marketing, selling or passing them to a third party — is prohibited under the Terms and may also be prohibited by law.

## 6. Transfer of Information Outside Israel

6.1. Our infrastructure providers operate servers outside Israel, including in the European Union and the United States. Information is therefore stored and processed outside Israel.

6.2. Such transfers are made in accordance with the Protection of Privacy Regulations (Transfer of Data to Databases Abroad), 5761‑2001, including by engaging providers that are subject to appropriate data protection rules or that commit to them by contract.

## 7. Information Security

7.1. We take reasonable security measures to protect the information, in accordance with the Protection of Privacy Regulations (Data Security), 5777‑2017, including: encryption in transit (TLS), passwords stored in hashed form only, user- and record-level access rules, activity logging, and limiting the Operator's team's access to what is necessary.

7.2. However, no system is completely secure. We cannot guarantee that the information is immune to any intrusion, unauthorized access or disruption, and we are not responsible for damage resulting from them, subject to the law.

7.3. Security incidents. In case of a severe security incident, we will act in accordance with the law, including reporting to the Privacy Protection Authority and notifying affected users, where required.

7.4. Your responsibility — keeping your password confidential, using a unique and strong password, locking your device, and notifying us immediately of any suspected misuse of your account.

## 8. How Long We Keep Information

8.1. We keep personal information for as long as your account is active and as long as needed for the purposes for which it was collected.

8.2. You can delete your account at any time through the account deletion screen in the App. After your account is deleted, we will delete or de-identify your personal information within a reasonable time, except for:

8.2.1. Information we must keep by law — for example accounting records and Brokerage Fee billing documents, kept for the period required by tax law (usually 7 years);

8.2.2. Information needed to defend against claims, to collect an open debt, or to resolve an open disagreement or dispute — until the matter is concluded or the relevant limitation period ends;

8.2.3. Limited information needed to prevent repeated breaches and the opening of circumvention accounts — for example a hash of the phone number, and the rating history and open charges of a deleted or blocked account, as set out in section 5.8 of the Terms;

8.2.4. Content published in shared spaces — messages you sent to other users, messages in Project Groups and Communities, and reviews you wrote — may remain visible to other users, usually without your identifying details;

8.2.5. Backups and technical logs, which are deleted in the regular backup cycle;

8.2.6. Aggregated and anonymous data that does not identify you.

## 9. Your Rights

9.1. You have the following rights, in accordance with the Privacy Protection Law:

9.1.1. Right of access — to review the personal information about you that we hold;

9.1.2. Correction or deletion of inaccurate information — to request that we correct or delete information that is not true, complete, clear or up to date;

9.1.3. Account deletion — to delete your account yourself in the App, or to ask us to do so, subject to the exceptions in chapter 8;

9.1.4. Removal from marketing — to ask to be removed from a marketing mailing list, at any time and unconditionally;

9.1.5. Withdrawal of consent — to withdraw consent you gave, without affecting the lawfulness of use made before. Withdrawing consent to processing needed to operate the service may require closing the account.

9.2. How to exercise your rights — contact us by email or through the "Contact Us" screen in the App, as set out in chapter 13. Some actions — such as editing your profile, deleting content and deleting your account — can be done yourself in the App.

9.3. Identification — to protect your privacy, we may ask for reasonable identity verification before handling a request.

9.4. Response time — we will respond to an access request within 30 days, and to other requests within a reasonable time and in accordance with the law. If we refuse a request, we will explain why, and you may appeal to the Magistrate's Court as provided by law.

9.5. Contacting the Authority — if you are not satisfied with our response, you may contact the Privacy Protection Authority at the Ministry of Justice.
