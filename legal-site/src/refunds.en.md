1.1. This Policy forms an integral part of the BAMA Terms of Use (the "Terms"). Terms not defined here have the meaning given to them in the Terms.

1.2. It is important to distinguish between two completely different payments:

|  | Payment for media services | Brokerage Fee |

1.4. No in-app purchases. The App does not offer purchases, subscriptions or payments through the Apple App Store or Google Play (section 12.13 of the Terms). Refund requests are therefore not handled through the app stores, only with us and under this Policy.

## 2. Cancelling an Engagement Between Client and Professional

2.1. The parties' arrangement prevails. Cancellation of an Engagement, its terms, cancellation fees and refund of advance payments are determined by the agreement between the Client and the Professional and by the law that applies to them. BAMA is not a party to that arrangement and does not enforce it.

2.2. Recommendation. Since cancellation is the most common cause of disputes, we strongly recommend agreeing in advance and in writing, before work begins, on:

2.2.1. The date-lock point and what happens if the work is cancelled after it;

2.2.2. A scale of cancellation fees by time before the shoot day (for example: cancellation up to 14 days in advance — no charge; 7–14 days — 25%; less than 7 days — 50%; on the day itself — 100%). These figures are an example only, and the parties are free to agree otherwise;

2.2.3. The amount of the advance payment and whether it is refundable;

2.2.4. What happens on cancellation due to force majeure, weather, illness or the security situation;

2.2.5. What happens if the Professional does not show up or does not deliver the deliverables.

2.3. Marking cancellation or withdrawal in the App. If an Engagement is cancelled, or the Professional withdraws from it, before the end date, this must be marked in the App promptly. An Engagement marked this way is not completed automatically and does not create a fee charge (sections 8.6.4 and 12.5 of the Terms).

2.4. If the cancellation was not marked. An Engagement that was actually cancelled but not marked as such in the App will be completed automatically on the Project end date (section 8.6.2 of the Terms), and a fee will be created for it if no dispute is submitted within the Dispute Period.

2.5. Early completion by the Client. A Client who marks the Project as completed before the end date thereby completes the Engagements of all Professionals hired for it (section 8.6.3 of the Terms). A Professional who did not actually do any work may notify in the App, within the Dispute Period, that the Engagement did not take place.

2.6. False marking. Marking an Engagement as cancelled, withdrawing from it, or notifying that it did not take place, when it was in fact performed, is a material breach of the Terms (section 12.9 of the Terms) and may create liability for the liquidated damages in section 12.10 of the Terms.

## 3. Non-delivery or Defective Performance by a Professional

3.1. Responsibility lies with the Professional. If a Professional does not provide the service, provides it late, or at a quality that does not match what was agreed — the Client's remedy is against the Professional only, under their agreement and the Contracts (Remedies for Breach of Contract) Law, 5731‑1970.

3.2. BAMA does not compensate, does not refund money and does not guarantee performance. The Operator has and will have no responsibility for payment made to the Professional that was not refunded.

3.3. What we can do. Following a well-founded report, we may, at our discretion: cancel the Brokerage Fee charge for that Engagement, or refund it to the Professional if already collected; change the Engagement's status; and in repeated or serious cases — suspend or block the Professional. Reviews are published, removed or suspended only under chapter 10 of the Terms.

3.4. Disputes are submitted through the App, subject to chapter 16 of the Terms. The decision is administrative and internal only, and is not arbitration.

## 4. Brokerage Fee — Cancellation and Refunds

4.1. When the charge is created. The Brokerage Fee charge is created at the end of the Dispute Period — 4 days from Engagement Completion, whether the Engagement was completed by the Professional's marking, by the Client's marking or automatically (sections 8.6 and 12.5 of the Terms). If a dispute is submitted in time, the charge is suspended until a decision is made.

4.1.1. A dispute is the main route. If the Engagement did not take place, or the amount on which the fee is calculated is wrong, submit a dispute in the App within the Dispute Period. The charge will then not be created until a decision is made, and no later refund request is needed.

4.1.2. Minimum fee. This chapter applies in the same way to the minimum fee in section 12.4.2 of the Terms. If a fee is cancelled or refunded under this Policy, it is cancelled or refunded in full, including the minimum component.

4.2. Cases in which no charge will be created, or the charge will be cancelled or refunded:

4.2.1. The Engagement was cancelled, or the Professional withdrew from it, before its completion;

4.2.2. Following a dispute, it was decided that the Engagement did not take place, or the amount was corrected — according to the decision;

4.2.3. A duplicate or incorrect charge on our part, including a calculation error;

4.2.4. A material fault in the App that prevented the Project from being carried out, or that prevented the Professional from submitting a dispute within the Dispute Period;

4.2.5. It was proven, through the dispute mechanism, that the service was not provided at all by the Professional;

4.2.6. The Engagement was completed, including by early completion by the Client, and the Professional could not submit a dispute in time for reasons beyond their control — provided the request is submitted within 14 days of Engagement Completion.

4.3. Cases in which the fee will not be cancelled or refunded:

4.3.1. The Engagement was performed in whole or in part, and the Professional received any payment for it;

4.3.2. The Client did not pay the Professional, although the service was provided — the Platform's service was provided in full, and collection from the Client is a matter between the parties (see also section 10.9 of the Terms);

4.3.3. The parties were satisfied, but the Professional changed their mind afterwards;

4.3.4. A disagreement about quality that does not amount to non-delivery;

4.3.5. No dispute was submitted within the Dispute Period, and there is no ground under section 4.2;

4.3.6. The request was submitted more than 14 days after the payment request, unless special circumstances justify otherwise.

4.4. Cancelling an unsettled charge. Since the Brokerage Fee is settled outside the App (section 12.6 of the Terms), in most cases the charge has not yet been paid when the request is made. In that case, the charge will be cancelled and removed from the Professional's balance, and no refund is needed.

4.5. How refunds are made, if paid. If the fee was actually paid and the refund is approved, it will be made by the same method used for payment (including through the payment provider), or by another method agreed with the Professional, within up to 14 business days of approval. Delays by the bank or payment provider may occur.

## 5. Store (BAMA MARKET / BAMA RENTAL) — Sale and Rental of Equipment

5.1. BAMA is not a party to the transaction. Cancellation, return of an item, warranty, refunds and rental terms are determined between the seller or lessor and the buyer or renter only.

5.2. BAMA does not refund money paid in a Store transaction, does not compensate for a defective, missing or undelivered item, and does not intermediate refunds.

5.3. Consumer rights. If the seller is a "dealer" and the buyer a "consumer" within the meaning of the Consumer Protection Law, 5741‑1981 — cancellation and refund rights under that law and the Consumer Protection Regulations (Cancellation of Transaction), 5771‑2010 may apply to the transaction. The obligation to comply with them lies with the seller only. In a transaction between two private individuals, consumer protection law usually does not apply, and the arrangement is contractual only.

5.4. We recommend physically inspecting an item before payment, documenting its condition on delivery and return, and setting out in writing the deposit, rental period and liability for damage, loss or theft.

## 6. Courses

6.1. Registration for a course, payment for it and any agreement regarding it are made with the course provider only and outside the App (chapter 13A of the Terms).

6.2. Cancelling a course registration and refunds for it are subject only to the course provider's terms and the law. BAMA does not refund money paid for a course and does not intermediate refunds.

## 7. Cancellation, Suspension or Blocking by the Operator

7.1. Blocking due to a breach. A User whose account was suspended or blocked due to a breach of the Terms or the law is not entitled to a refund of fees paid, and the suspension does not cancel charges created before it.

7.2. Blocking not due to a breach. If an account is discontinued at the Operator's initiative and not due to a breach by the User — no fees will be charged for Engagements that could not be completed because of the discontinuation, and such charges will be cancelled.

7.3. Discontinuation of the App. If the App is discontinued, no fees will be charged for Engagements that were not completed, and open charges created for them will be cancelled.

7.4. Deleting your account. Deleting your account does not cancel charges created up to the date of deletion (section 5.7 of the Terms).

## 8. How to Submit a Request

8.1. A dispute about Engagement Completion or the fee amount is submitted in the App itself, within the Dispute Period (section 8.6.5 of the Terms). This chapter applies to refund requests and appeals against a charge.

8.2. A refund request or appeal against a charge should be sent by email or through the "Contact Us" screen in the App, and should include:

8.2.1. Your user name and account;

8.2.2. The Project or Engagement ID, or the payment request number and date;

8.2.3. The amount charged;

8.2.4. Details of the grounds for the request;

8.2.5. Any relevant evidence — screenshots, correspondence, payment confirmations.

8.3. Response time. We will respond to a request within 14 business days. If we reject a request, we will state the reasons.

8.4. Appeal. A decision on a refund request may be appealed once, by a further request within 14 days of receiving the decision, with new reasons or evidence.

8.5. Nothing in this chapter affects your right to go to a competent court or to file a complaint with the competent authority.
